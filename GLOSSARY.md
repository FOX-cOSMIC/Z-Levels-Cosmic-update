# Z-Levels

Z-Levels adds stacked maps to RimWorld. The surface, the lower level, and the upper level are linked by stairs and power, and Rimatomics cooling can now cross the levels too. This glossary defines the terms those features share.

## Levels

**Surface**:
The normal map the colony starts on.
_Avoid_: ground, main map

**Lower**:
The underground level below the surface.
_Avoid_: underground, basement

**Upper**:
The level built above the surface.
_Avoid_: sky, above

**Stairs**:
The buildings that let a colonist walk between two levels.
_Avoid_: ladder, ramp

## Cross-level links

**Pairing**:
A link between two columns on adjacent levels. Cooling columns pair only when they stand on the same cell (ADR-0005).
_Avoid_: linking, connection

**Power transmitter column**:
A column that carries power across a pairing.
_Avoid_: pylon, power pole

**Cooling column**:
A vertical coolant pipe that pairs with a cooling column on an adjacent level, so the two levels share one cooling loop.
_Avoid_: cooling transmitter, cooling tower

**Partner highlight**:
The outline on one level that marks a cell where a cooling column on an adjacent level pairs.
_Avoid_: link line, beam, connection line

## Cooling

**Cooling loop**:
The coolers, turbines, and coolant pipes joined into one circuit, including the parts on paired levels.
_Avoid_: cooling net, cooling system

**Cooler**:
A Rimatomics building that takes heat out of a cooling loop.
_Avoid_: chiller, cooling system

**Turbine**:
A Rimatomics building that converts a cooling loop's heat into power.
_Avoid_: generator

**Coolant pipe**:
A Rimatomics pipe that carries coolant between coolers and turbines.
_Avoid_: cooling pipe, water pipe

## Reactor effects

**Radiation**:
Harm that a damaged reactor core sends to colonists and things on its own level.
_Avoid_: rads

**Fallout**:
A lingering radioactive condition that a meltdown leaves on its level.
_Avoid_: radiation

**Meltdown**:
A reactor failure that explodes and leaves radiation and fallout on its level.
_Avoid_: explosion, core breach
